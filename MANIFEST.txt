Small delta on top of v0.4.3. Only files changed since that delivery are

WHAT WAS FIXED, BY REQUEST
-----------------------------
1. "The legend overlaps with the y-axis" (top-left growth curve panel):
   The legend used matplotlib's automatic "best" placement, which was
   landing right on top of the y-axis label and tick numbers. Moved to a
   fixed loc="lower right" -- reliably empty, since at high T the curve
   rises well above the data cloud. Verified with an actual bounding-box
   overlap check (not just "looks fine"): legend vs. y-axis label/ticks,
   confirmed False.

2. "Not possible to read the station names due to overlapping" (L-moment
   ratio diagram): station labels collide when stations cluster tightly
   in (t3, t4) space -- which is exactly the case for a well-pooling,
   homogeneous region, i.e. the case you most want to read clearly. Added
   a small no-dependency staggering helper: labels are ordered by
   L-kurtosis and given a monotonically increasing vertical offset,
   alternating left/right of the point, connected back to the actual data
   point with a thin leader line. Verified by isolating the text glyph
   bounding boxes from the leader-line arrows (which inflate a naive bbox
   check) and confirming zero pairwise overlaps.

3. "Add the 10,000-year growth factor to the summary": done -- the
   dashboard's text panel now lists T=10/100/1000/10,000-year growth
   factors instead of just the first three.
